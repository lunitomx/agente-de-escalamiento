# VisionHub Accelerator comparison evidence — 2026-08-19

## Provenance

- Surface: `https://visionhub.com.mx/accelerator/diagnostico/`
- Browser: user-authorized Chrome tab
- Run: real filled diagnostic, advanced to the result, no PDF download or
  additional edits
- Privacy: raw personal, company, and financial answers are not copied here

## Observed flow

1. Promise and instructions before intake: purpose, confidentiality, expected
   outputs, and permission to estimate.
2. Company calibration: identity, industry, years, revenue, projection,
   collaborators, margin, leadership, and operating-vs-directing time.
3. Five operating pillars: Gente, Estrategia, Comercial, Ejecución, Efectivo.
   Each uses a labeled 1–5 scale; several items add open text seeded from a
   one-page plan.
4. Commercial evidence: last-month prospect sources, conversations,
   proposals, wins, optional average sale, and a plain-language reason to buy.
5. Separate owner variable: “Tú” is reported outside the five pillars.
6. Context prompts: elephant in the room, two opportunities, obstacle,
   strength, non-money outcome, 12-month outcome, 5/10-year view, and strategic
   priorities.
7. Result: global and pillar scores, system-vs-result comparison, funnel
   conversion, bottleneck with three supporting response rows, quarterly route,
   learning-day minimums, accountability prompts, candidate metrics, plan
   prefill, and a local-looking workshop handoff.

## Product lessons

### Borrow

- Tell the user what they will receive before asking for effort.
- Combine structured facts with a small number of high-value open prompts.
- Show evidence behind the bottleneck instead of only naming a low score.
- Turn the result into a constrained route with a cadence, owner, and metric.
- Treat an existing plan as a useful prefill, not as an unquestionable fact.

### Do not copy

- A long mandatory wall of questions before any insight.
- Hidden scoring until the final action.
- A zero score for “no sellers” when the question is not applicable.
- Mixed currencies, dates, or planning horizons without normalization and
  provenance.
- A strong prescription without showing confidence, denominator, or what must
  be validated in the workshop.
