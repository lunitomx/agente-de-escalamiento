# S49.1 Design

## Approach

Add a small Pydantic contract in `coaching/diagnose/models.py` and export it
from `coaching.diagnose`. Reuse the existing local-locator safety rule from
`coaching.evidence.models`, but keep the new model independent enough for
diagnostic answers and future result consumers.

## Components

| Component | Change |
|-----------|--------|
| `coaching/diagnose/models.py` | New typed evidence/intake models |
| `coaching/diagnose/__init__.py` | Export models without changing `run()` contract |
| `coaching/diagnose/tests/test_contract.py` | New TDD coverage |

## Decisions

- Use explicit literals for applicability/status/confidence/freshness.
- Compute `included_in_score` from applicability and answered state.
- Keep `source_ref` relative/local; reject URLs and absolute paths.

## Acceptance

The model can be constructed, dumped to JSON, and rejects invalid boundaries
without touching filesystem or network state.
