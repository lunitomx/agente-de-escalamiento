# Architecture Review — S49.3

## Findings

### Critical

None. The builder is pure and the typed funnel is a narrow contract consumed by
the intake and future scoring path.

### Recommended

- Keep file/CRM readers outside the builder; they should produce evidence items
  rather than introduce persistence or network coupling here (H14 guard).
- Retain the compatibility `__getitem__` only through the migration window and
  remove it once consumers use `FunnelMetrics` explicitly.

## Verdict

**PASS WITH QUESTIONS** — the removal timing for compatibility access is
deferred to consumer migration.
