# S49.1 Scope

## In scope

- Pydantic models for one answer/evidence item and diagnostic intake metadata.
- Applicability, answer status, freshness, confidence, provenance, and stable ID.
- Safe local locator validation and score-inclusion semantics.
- Unit tests for valid, invalid, stale, estimated, and N/A cases.

## Out of scope

- Conversation routing, scoring aggregation, export rendering, and persistence.
- Hosted data transfer or telemetry.

## Done when

- [ ] Contract is importable from `coaching.diagnose`.
- [ ] N/A is distinct from a numeric zero.
- [ ] Unsafe locators fail closed.
- [ ] Targeted tests, lint, formatting, and type checks pass.
