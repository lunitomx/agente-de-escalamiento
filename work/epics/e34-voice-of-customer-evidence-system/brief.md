# E34 Brief: Voice of Customer Evidence System

**Status:** Planned
**Created:** 2026-06-18
**Source drafts:** E20 Voice of Customer Evidence Capture, E19 Strategy Core Skills

## Hypothesis

If ScaleUp captures real customer voice before defining Core Customer, Brand
Promise, positioning, and Strategy Canvas outputs, strategy guidance will be
less assumption-driven and easier to audit.

This epic revives the valuable part of draft E20 and absorbs the useful part of
draft E19 as strategy hardening. The strategy prompts should improve only after
the evidence contract exists.

## Success Metrics

- Customer evidence records include source, context, date, quote/content, and
  review status.
- Core Customer and Brand Promise work can cite evidence records instead of
  unsupported claims.
- Strategy prompt hardening keeps one-question-per-turn flow and uses evidence
  before recommendation.
- A reusable evidence library exists for downstream strategy skills.
- Tests or validators reject evidence-free strategic claims where the flow
  requires customer proof.

## Appetite

Medium. The first version should prove the evidence contract and strategy
handoff. It should not become a CRM, sentiment platform, or broad automation
system.

## Rabbit Holes

- Importing external review platforms before the local evidence schema is
  stable.
- Rewriting the entire strategy course instead of hardening the narrow prompts
  that consume evidence.
- Treating unverified anecdotes as equivalent to sourced customer evidence.
- Building UI before the data contract and validation rules exist.
