# S34.3 Story: Voice-To-Strategy Mapping

## User Story

As a strategy facilitator, I want customer evidence mapped into Core Customer,
Brand Promise, and positioning inputs so strategic claims are traceable.

## Acceptance Criteria

### Scenario: Evidence Produces Cited Strategy Inputs

Given approved Voice of Customer records with relevant evidence tags
When the records are mapped into strategy inputs
Then the output includes Core Customer, Brand Promise, and positioning inputs
And every generated input cites evidence ids.

### Scenario: Insufficient Evidence Produces Gaps

Given no approved evidence for a required strategy category
When the records are mapped into strategy inputs
Then the output reports an explicit gap
And no unsupported strategy claim is generated.

### Scenario: Contradictory Evidence Remains Visible

Given approved evidence with positive and negative signals for the same category
When the records are mapped into strategy inputs
Then both signals remain visible for review
And the output does not collapse them into one unsupported conclusion.
