# S34.1 Story: Evidence Intake Schema

## User Story

As a ScaleUp operator, I want a strict evidence record format so customer voice
can be captured with enough provenance to support strategic decisions.

## Acceptance Criteria

### Scenario: Complete Customer Evidence Passes

Given a customer evidence record with source type, source label, captured date,
context, quote, customer segment, evidence tags, and review status
When the record is validated
Then validation passes
And the record is usable for strategy claims.

### Scenario: Missing Provenance Is Not Strategy-Usable

Given a customer evidence record without source, context, or captured date
When the record is validated
Then validation fails or marks the record not usable for strategy claims
And the validation output explains the missing provenance.

### Scenario: Synthetic Fixture Is Explicit

Given a synthetic customer evidence fixture
When the record is validated
Then it is accepted only when explicitly marked as fixture data
And it cannot be confused with real customer evidence.

## Examples

### Valid Real Evidence

```yaml
id: voc-001
source_type: interview
source_label: Customer interview with Ana Ruiz
captured_at: "2026-06-01"
context: Renewal conversation after 90 days using the service.
quote: "I stayed because the team made cash decisions simple every week."
customer_segment: founder-led services company
evidence_tags:
  - cash
  - clarity
review_status: approved
is_fixture: false
notes: Approved for strategy use by operator.
```

### Invalid For Strategy Claims

```yaml
id: voc-002
source_type: note
quote: "They liked the process."
review_status: draft
is_fixture: false
```
