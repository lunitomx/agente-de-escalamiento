# S34.3 Plan: Voice-To-Strategy Mapping

**Story:** S34.3 Voice-To-Strategy Mapping
**Size:** M
**Date:** 2026-06-19

## Overview

Create a deterministic mapping layer from approved Voice of Customer evidence
records to cited strategy inputs. The mapper must surface gaps and
contradictions instead of generating unsupported strategy prose.

## Risks And Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Mapper becomes a strategy copy generator. | Return structured inputs only; final prose stays out of scope. |
| Tag mapping is too magical. | Use explicit deterministic tag aliases and tests. |
| Draft/fixture evidence creates claims. | Filter by `strategy_usable` before producing inputs. |
