# S34.2 Story: Testimonial Normalization

## User Story

As a ScaleUp operator, I want raw quotes and notes normalized into evidence
records so customer voice is reusable without losing source context.

## Acceptance Criteria

### Scenario: Complete Raw Quote Normalizes

Given a raw customer quote with source, context, captured date, customer
segment, and tags
When it is normalized
Then a valid `CustomerEvidenceRecord` is produced
And the raw quote text is preserved.

### Scenario: Missing Context Is Preserved As A Gap

Given a raw customer quote without source label, context, or captured date
When it is normalized
Then no missing provenance is invented
And the output is not strategy-usable.

### Scenario: Duplicate Quote Is Detected

Given two raw quotes with the same source label and quote text
When they are normalized as a batch
Then the duplicate is reported or rejected
And only distinct evidence records are returned.

## Examples

```yaml
- quote: "The weekly cash review helped us decide faster."
  source_type: interview
  source_label: Founder interview with Maria
  captured_at: "2026-06-02"
  context: Cash acceleration follow-up.
  customer_segment: founder-led services company
  evidence_tags:
    - cash
    - speed
```
