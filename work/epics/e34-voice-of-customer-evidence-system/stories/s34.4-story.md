# S34.4 Story: Strategy Prompt Hardening

## User Story

As a ScaleUp user, I want strategy prompts to ask for and use customer evidence
before forming Core Customer, Brand Promise, or Strategy Canvas outputs.

## Acceptance Criteria

### Scenario: Evidence Gate Before Strategy Claims

Given a strategy skill is about to guide Core Customer, Brand Promise, OPSP, or
7 Strata decisions
When Voice of Customer evidence is missing or has mapping gaps
Then the skill asks for the missing evidence first
And it does not invent a strategy claim.

### Scenario: Cited Inputs Are Required

Given `map_evidence_to_strategy` produces strategy inputs
When the skill guides strategy work
Then Core Customer, Brand Promise, positioning, and Strategy Canvas guidance
references evidence ids from those inputs.

### Scenario: One-Question Flow Preserved

Given the user needs to provide more evidence
When the skill asks a follow-up
Then it asks one question at a time
And keeps existing strategy command names recognizable.
