# S34.3 Scope: Voice-To-Strategy Mapping

## In Scope

- Define strategy mapping categories for customer segment, desired outcome,
  pain, promised value, objection, proof, and reusable language.
- Generate strategy input summaries that cite evidence ids.
- Report gaps when evidence is insufficient.
- Preserve contradictory positive/negative evidence for review.
- Keep mapping deterministic and fixture-based.

## Out of Scope

- Final strategy prose generation.
- UI dashboards.
- Automated customer segmentation.
- Prompt hardening for strategy skills.
- Production evidence storage.

## Done When

- Approved evidence records produce cited strategy inputs.
- Draft/fixture evidence is excluded from strategy claims.
- Missing evidence produces gaps instead of claims.
- Contradictory evidence is visible in mapping output.
- Tests cover cited mappings, evidence gaps, and contradiction preservation.
