- Implementing E35 golden cases.

- A canonical handoff doc exists outside old E19/E20 drafts.
- Tests prove the handoff references the implemented API and examples.
- E34 final audit distinguishes real evidence from fixtures.
- E34 retrospective lists completed stories, gates, risks, and follow-ups.
- E34 scope progress marks S34.5 complete and status is corrected from Planned
  to Complete only after evidence exists.
