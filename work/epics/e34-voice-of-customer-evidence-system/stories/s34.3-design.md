# S34.3 Design: Voice-To-Strategy Mapping

## Complexity

Moderate. This extends the Voice of Customer validator with deterministic
mapping models and adds a new focused test file.

## Problem

Validated Voice of Customer records exist after S34.1/S34.2, but strategy
skills still need a structured way to consume them. Without a mapping layer,
Core Customer, Brand Promise, and positioning can still become unsupported
claims.

## Value

S34.3 turns approved customer evidence into cited strategy inputs and explicit
gaps. This makes the next prompt-hardening story concrete: strategy prompts can
require cited inputs instead of asking generally for "evidence."

## Gemba Findings

- `validators/voice_of_customer.py` owns the evidence model and normalization
  result; mapping belongs there for now.
- `scaleup-strategy-opsp` asks for Brand Promise and sandbox/customer segment,
  but does not cite customer evidence.
- `scaleup-strategy-7strata` asks for Brand Promise, Words you own, and
  One-Phrase Strategy, but has no evidence contract.
- No existing deterministic Voice of Customer strategy mapper was found.

## Legacy Sweep

Nothing becomes orphaned. This is a new mapping layer over the S34.1/S34.2
contract.

## Approach

Add deterministic mapping structures to `validators/voice_of_customer.py`:

- `StrategyCategory`
- `StrategyInput`
- `StrategyGap`
- `StrategyContradiction`
- `StrategyMappingResult`
- `map_evidence_to_strategy(records)`

Tag-based mapping rules:

- `segment` or `core-customer` -> customer segment.
- `outcome`, `desired-outcome`, `job-to-be-done` -> desired outcome.
- `pain`, `friction`, `problem` -> pain.
- `promise`, `value`, `brand-promise` -> promised value.
- `objection`, `risk`, `negative` -> objection.
- `proof`, `result`, `metric` -> proof.
- `language`, `phrase`, `words` -> reusable language.

Only `strategy_usable` records can generate inputs. Draft, rejected, or fixture
records can inform gaps later, but they must not produce claims in this story.

## Components

| Component | Change | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `validators/voice_of_customer.py` | modify | Add strategy mapping models and deterministic mapper. |
| `tests/test_voice_of_customer_mapping.py` | create | Mapping, gaps, and contradiction tests. |
| `tests/fixtures/voice_of_customer/strategy_mapping.yaml` | create | Fixture evidence for mapping scenarios. |

## Acceptance Criteria

### MUST

- Approved evidence produces strategy inputs with cited evidence ids.
- Fixture/draft evidence is excluded from strategy claims.
- Missing categories produce explicit gaps.
- Contradictory positive/negative evidence remains visible.

### MUST NOT

- Do not generate final strategy prose.
- Do not edit strategy skill prompts in this story.
- Do not introduce model-based semantic classification.

## Testing Strategy

| Layer | Test | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Unit | approved evidence maps to cited inputs | Proves main value. |
| Unit | fixture/draft evidence excluded | Proves no weak evidence becomes a claim. |
| Unit | insufficient categories produce gaps | Proves gaps instead of invented claims. |
| Unit | contradictory evidence remains visible | Proves review safety. |
