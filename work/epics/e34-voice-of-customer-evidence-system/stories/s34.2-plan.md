**Story:** S34.2 Testimonial Normalization
**Size:** M

## Overview

Extend the S34.1 evidence contract with a minimal raw quote normalizer. The
normalizer must preserve missing provenance as a review gap and must not produce
strategy-usable evidence unless provenance is complete and approved.
