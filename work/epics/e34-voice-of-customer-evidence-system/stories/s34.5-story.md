# S34.5 Story: Evidence Library Handoff

## User Story

As a future skill author, I want a reusable evidence library contract so other
skills can load and cite Voice of Customer records consistently.

## Acceptance Criteria

### Scenario: Contract Is Discoverable

Given a future story needs the Voice of Customer evidence contract
When it searches project docs
Then it can find the contract without reading old E19/E20 draft folders.

### Scenario: Consumer Can Cite Evidence

Given a strategy skill consumes mapped Voice of Customer evidence
When it produces a strategy input
Then examples show how to cite evidence ids and handle gaps.

### Scenario: Epic Closure Distinguishes Real Evidence From Fixtures

Given E34 used test fixtures during implementation
When the epic closes
Then final audit states which evidence was fixture data
And does not imply fixture data is real customer evidence.
