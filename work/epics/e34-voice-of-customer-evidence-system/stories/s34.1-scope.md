# S34.1 Scope: Evidence Intake Schema

## In Scope

- Define the local storage location for evidence fixtures and records.
- Define a typed evidence record with required provenance fields.
- Add validation for real records versus synthetic fixtures.
- Add test fixtures for valid, incomplete, and fixture-labeled evidence.
- Expose validation results that explain whether a record is usable for
  strategy claims.

## Out of Scope

- CRM import/export.
- Audio transcription.
- Testimonial normalization from raw unstructured notes.
- Voice-to-strategy mapping.
- Strategy prompt hardening.

## Done When

- A complete customer evidence record validates successfully.
- A real record missing source, context, or captured date is not strategy-usable.
- Synthetic examples must be explicitly labeled as fixtures.
- Tests cover valid evidence, missing provenance, fixture labeling, and invalid
  enum/status values.
- Story artifacts include design, plan, progress, and retrospective before
  story close.
