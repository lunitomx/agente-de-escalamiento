# S34.4 Design: Strategy Prompt Hardening

## Complexity

Moderate. This is documentation/prompt behavior hardening across mirrored
strategy skill files plus tests that prevent evidence-first rules from drifting.

## Problem

S34.1-S34.3 created a Voice of Customer evidence contract, normalization, and
mapping. The strategy skills still ask for Brand Promise, Sandbox, 7 Strata, and
OPSP inputs without requiring cited evidence first.

## Value

Strategy prompts become evidence-first. The agent must ask for or cite Voice of
Customer evidence before forming Core Customer, Brand Promise, positioning, or
Strategy Canvas claims.

## Gemba Findings

- `.agents/skills/scaleup-strategy/SKILL.md` and `.claude/skills/scaleup-strategy/SKILL.md`
  are currently identical and route to OPSP/7 Strata.
- OPSP asks for Brand Promise and Sandbox but does not require VoC evidence.
- 7 Strata asks for Words you own, Sandbox, Brand Promise, and One-Phrase
  Strategy but does not require evidence ids.
- SWOT informs OPSP but does not itself form Core Customer or Brand Promise; it
  remains out of scope for S34.4.

## Legacy Sweep

No V1 becomes orphaned. Existing strategy skills keep their command names and
flow; S34.4 adds an evidence gate and one-question behavior.

## Approach

Patch the mirrored `.agents` and `.claude` files for:

- `scaleup-strategy`
- `scaleup-strategy-opsp`
- `scaleup-strategy-7strata`

Each touched skill gets a Voice of Customer evidence section that:

- references `CustomerEvidenceRecord` and `map_evidence_to_strategy`;
- requires cited evidence ids before Core Customer, Brand Promise, positioning,
  Strategy Canvas, OPSP, or 7 Strata claims;
- instructs the agent to ask one missing-evidence question at a time;
- forbids inventing claims when mapping gaps exist.

## Components

| Component | Change | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `.agents/skills/scaleup-strategy*/SKILL.md` | modify | Evidence-first strategy guidance. |
| `.claude/skills/scaleup-strategy*/SKILL.md` | modify | Keep mirrored skill docs aligned. |
| `tests/test_strategy_voice_of_customer_prompting.py` | create | Guard evidence-first prompt hardening and mirror alignment. |

## Acceptance Criteria

### MUST

- Strategy entrypoint mentions `map_evidence_to_strategy`.
- OPSP requires evidence ids before Brand Promise and Sandbox/customer claims.
- 7 Strata requires evidence ids before Words you own, Brand Promise, and
  One-Phrase Strategy.
- Missing evidence prompts one question at a time.
- `.agents` and `.claude` touched skill docs remain identical.

### MUST NOT

- Do not rewrite the entire strategy course.
- Do not change skill command names.
- Do not add new strategy framework concepts.

## Testing Strategy

| Layer | Test | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Doc contract | entrypoint evidence gate present | Proves strategy starts evidence-first. |
| Doc contract | OPSP hardening present | Proves Brand Promise/Sandbox require evidence ids. |
| Doc contract | 7 Strata hardening present | Proves differentiation claims require evidence ids. |
| Drift | mirror equality | Proves `.agents` and `.claude` do not diverge. |
