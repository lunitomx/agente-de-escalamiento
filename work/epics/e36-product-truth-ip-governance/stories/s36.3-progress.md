## T1 Evidence

- The policy is deny-first and defaults every unmatched path to
  `not_eligible`; a passing canary is not an export allowlist.
- Strict frozen models reject unknown fields, unsafe paths/globs, duplicate or
  ambiguous same-disposition rules, reversed precedence, and missing raw,
  derived, private, or internal deny classes.
- The S36.2 baseline is bound by byte hash, scan status, policy hash, verifier
  commit, and three independent raw-reference surface states.
- Findings serialize rule/code/surface/safe locator only; grouped summaries
  omit locators and values.
- Generic business vocabulary remains outside the prohibited term policy.
- No new dependency, hosted component, database, telemetry, remote mutation,
  or public-candidate write was introduced.

## T2 Evidence

- Real temporary Git repositories prove worktree, canonical `HEAD`, reachable
  path history, and dirty public-candidate `HEAD` remain independent surfaces.
- Read-only Git subprocesses remove inherited `GIT_DIR`, `GIT_WORK_TREE`, and
  `GIT_CONFIG_*` overrides; strong fingerprints match before and after scans.
- Candidate-public text, path identities, and root YAML provenance are checked
  case-insensitively without serializing matched values; prohibited paths use
  SHA-256 locators everywhere.
- Denied and `not_eligible` content is classified but never promoted or opened
  by the private canary.
- Binary, symlink, non-UTF-8, oversized, missing, and malformed required content
  fails closed with typed safe evidence.
- The public candidate is scanned from committed blobs only. Its dirty/untracked
  sentinel content is ignored and its legacy findings remain explicitly
  unresolved rather than becoming a clean claim.
- JSON is deterministic machine evidence; Markdown is grouped and bounded.
  Writers and the CLI create outputs only for a passing private boundary, while
  failures emit one fixed sentence.
