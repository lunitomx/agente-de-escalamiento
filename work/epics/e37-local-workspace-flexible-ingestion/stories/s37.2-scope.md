## In Scope

- Typed local source identity, fingerprint and relative provenance contracts.
- A small registry of local adapters/capability declarations for CSV, TSV,
  XLSX, text documents, PDF and transcript inputs.
- Structural profiling of supported tabular/text sources: candidate sheets or
  tables, header rows, units, dates and entity columns.
- Deterministic `ready`, `needs_clarification`, `unsupported` and
  `provider_unavailable` outcomes with bounded question codes and choices.
- Rerun-safe receipts that never include absolute machine paths, file contents,
  secrets or cloud/API metadata.
- TDD fixtures for valid, ambiguous, changed-byte, corrupt, encrypted and
  unknown-extension inputs.

## Out of Scope

- SQLite schema changes or canonical business-state writes; S37.1 remains the
  only authority guard.
- Inbox watching, deduplication across runs, quarantine moves and retry policy;
  those belong to S37.3.
- Financial reconstruction, cash coaching, dailies/weeklies analysis,
  strategy, dashboard or executive diagnosis (E38-E40).
- OCR, semantic transcription correction, arbitrary universal parsing or
  network/cloud APIs, OAuth, hosted execution and telemetry.

## Done when

- [ ] A typed registry reports capabilities and explicit safe failure reasons
      for every declared input family.
- [ ] CSV/TSV and at least one workbook/text path are profiled from real local
      fixtures without requiring a new template.
- [ ] Material ambiguity produces deterministic questions and prevents any
      canonical state mutation.
- [ ] Fingerprint, relative provenance and source identity are byte-stable on
      rerun and change when source bytes change.
- [ ] Receipts are deterministic and redacted; absolute paths and source
      contents are proven absent by tests.
- [ ] Focused tests, full tests, lint, format and Pyright pass, and a story
      retrospective is committed before close.
