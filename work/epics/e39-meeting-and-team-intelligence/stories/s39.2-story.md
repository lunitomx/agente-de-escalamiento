# S39.2: Evidence Extraction and Rhythm

## User story

Como director, quiero que ESCALA convierta lo explícito de cada transcript en decisiones, acciones y señales con evidencia, y que pueda revisar si el ritmo declarado tiene soporte sin castigar a nadie por un archivo que no llegó.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given un transcript con etiquetas explícitas, when se extrae, then decisions/actions/owners/due dates/blockers/risks/commitments have line provenance and confidence.
- Given an action with an owner or due date, when se analiza, then those fields remain attached to the fact or unresolved with a question.
- Given a declared daily/weekly rhythm and a date window, when se evalúa, then observed/missing evidence is shown and person impact remains `not_assessed`.
- Given a changed source after intake, when se extrae, then the result blocks instead of analyzing bytes under stale provenance.

## Out of scope

Cross-meeting trends, executive review, scheduling and report export.
