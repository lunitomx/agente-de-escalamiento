# S39.3: Temporal Team Signals and Executive Review

## User story

Como director, quiero comparar mis reuniones a través del tiempo para ver blockers repetidos, compromisos vencidos y decisiones abiertas, y recibir una revisión diaria basada solo en la evidencia que entregué.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given multiple meeting records, when se agregan, then repeated blockers and commitments are detected by normalized value and source evidence.
- Given an action/commitment with a due date before the review date, when se revisa, then it is marked overdue with its source evidence.
- Given an empty or unresolved record set, when se revisa, then the health state is evidence-limited/unresolved and no negative finding is invented.
- Given a sequence with changing fact frequency, when se analiza, then a cross-meeting trend signal includes the compared source IDs and counts.
- Given a daily executive review, when se genera, then it includes health, material changes, questions and supporting evidence.

## Out of scope

Writing report files, scheduling, DISC, psychological inference and cloud integrations.
