## In scope

- Contratos Pydantic para meeting context, provenance y questions.
- Intake idempotente basado en `build_source_identity` y ledger JSON local.
- Detección acotada de meeting type, date, team y participants mencionados.
- Receipts deterministas y redacted.

## Out of scope

Fact extraction, temporal trend analysis, scheduler and HTML report.

## Done when

- Focused tests prove success, duplicate, ambiguity, unsupported input and no mutation.
- `escala_server.meetings` exports the intake seam without changing E37 behavior.
- Story retrospective and local merge commit exist.
