# Strategy Decision Overview

## Purpose
Strategy answers the question: "Can you state your firm's strategy simply — and is it driving sustainable revenue growth?"

## Core Concepts
- **Core Values**: the non-negotiable behaviors that define culture.
- **Purpose**: why the company exists beyond profit.
- **BHAG**: the long-term, audacious destination.
- **Brand Promise**: a measurable promise to the target customer.
- **Sandbox**: the defined competitive arena.
- **Differentiating Activities**: what the company does differently to sustain advantage.

## Tools
- One-Page Strategic Plan (OPSP)
- 7 Strata of Strategy
- SWT Analysis (Strengths, Weaknesses, Trends)

## When to Use
Use Strategy tools when the leader needs clarity on direction, differentiation, or long-term priorities.

## Readiness Check
- Core Values defined? If no → start with Core Values Discovery.
- Revenue growth unclear or stalled? → use 7 Strata or SWT.
- Strategy ready to commit? → build/update OPSP.
